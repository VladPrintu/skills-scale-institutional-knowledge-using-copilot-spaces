# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX / UI Designers or User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX / UI Designers and User Researchers translate customer needs into usable interfaces, flows, and validation feedback. They help teams build work that is intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user interviews, usability testing, and workflow analysis
- Define UX requirements, interaction patterns, and accessibility expectations
- Review prototypes and designs with Product Managers and Developers
- Validate whether features meet user needs and business outcomes
- Surface usability risks early and propose improvements

### Goals
- Improve usability, clarity, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework caused by design ambiguity or missed user needs
- Ensure accessibility and inclusive product experiences

### Typical Communication
- Discovery interviews and research summaries
- Design reviews and prototype walkthroughs
- Feedback loops with Product Managers, Developers, and QA

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers on problem framing, user needs, and acceptance criteria
- Collaborates with Developers on implementation trade-offs and edge cases
- Shares findings with Stakeholders to support prioritization and evidence-based decisions
- Coordinates with QA on usability and accessibility validation scenarios

---

## Technical Leads or Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, design decisions, and solution integrity. They ensure the team chooses approaches that are maintainable, scalable, and aligned with delivery goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define architectural patterns, integration points, and technical standards
- Review major technical decisions, trade-offs, and dependency risks
- Coordinate technical delivery across multiple workstreams or teams
- Support Developers on design and implementation quality
- Help identify and mitigate technical debt and platform risks

### Goals
- Deliver robust, maintainable, and scalable solutions
- Reduce delivery risk caused by unclear technical direction
- Align engineering decisions with product goals and operational realities

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Dependency planning and technical risk updates
- Escalation points with Project Managers and Product Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to translate business outcomes into feasible technical options
- Guides Developers on trade-offs, design quality, and implementation consistency
- Advises Project Managers on technical dependencies, sequencing, and delivery risk
- Works with QA to ensure testability, observability, and release readiness

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads protect the team from avoidable risk by identifying requirements, controls, and compliance needs early. They help the project stay safe, trustworthy, and ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Assess security and privacy risks for features, data handling, and integrations
- Define required security controls, review points, and compliance checkpoints
- Partner with Developers during design and implementation to reduce risk
- Support release readiness reviews and incident-prep planning
- Escalate high-risk issues to the appropriate stakeholders and owners

### Goals
- Minimize security and privacy exposure
- Improve trust and compliance for customers and stakeholders
- Catch risks before they become expensive late-stage problems

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and risk triage
- Privacy impact discussions and feature checklists
- Release readiness and exception tracking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads during planning and implementation
- Collaborates with QA on validation of controls, privacy requirements, and regressions
- Coordinates with Project Managers on escalation paths, timelines, and deployment risk
- Shares risk updates with Stakeholders where business or compliance decisions are needed

---

## Data / Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Owners make sure the team can measure whether a project is delivering value. They define the metrics, instrumentation, and reporting needed to evaluate outcomes and guide iteration.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and instrumentation requirements
- Review data quality, measurement gaps, and reporting needs
- Work with Developers to implement tracking and validation logic
- Interpret usage and performance signals for decision-making
- Connect project outcomes to stakeholder reporting and business goals

### Goals
- Make outcomes measurable and visible
- Improve learning cycles from usage data and product signals
- Keep measurement aligned with business value and user experience

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and dashboard reviews
- Reporting updates for stakeholders and product reviews
- Feedback loops with Product Managers and PMs on progress against goals

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on success metrics and product decisions
- Works with Developers to ensure tracking is implemented correctly and consistently
- Shares trends and measurement gaps with Stakeholders and Project Managers
- Supports QA by validating that essential metrics and flows can be observed and tested

---

## Release / Operations Owners

### Role Summary
Release and Operations Owners ensure projects move safely into production and remain observable after launch. They coordinate deployment hygiene, operational readiness, and rapid recovery when issues arise.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release timing, deployment checks, and communication plans
- Prepare rollback, incident, and mitigation steps
- Monitor service health and user impact after deployment
- Align release readiness with QA, Product, and engineering needs
- Support operational accountability for production quality and continuity

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and production incidents
- Improve deployment confidence and recovery speed
- Keep delivery teams aligned on operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness checks and deployment briefings
- Post-deploy verification and incident updates
- Stakeholder communication during high-impact launches

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Developers and QA on verification and rollback readiness
- Supports Project Managers on scheduling, communication, and escalation timing
- Aligns with Product Managers to confirm launch criteria and stakeholder expectations
- Helps Stakeholders understand operational trade-offs and risk levels

---

## Role Flexibility for Smaller Teams

In smaller teams, one person may carry more than one of these responsibilities. The goal is not to create rigid silos, but to clarify ownership and decision-making. When roles are combined, the team should still document:
- which responsibilities are primary
- who is accountable for escalation and approval
- how decisions are communicated across the project
- where work should be handed off during handoff points or incidents

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The project team can assign multiple responsibilities to the same person when the team is small, while still keeping clear ownership and escalation paths.
